# Media-kit metrics verified 15 October 2026

Source: the account owner's signed-in [Instagram Professional dashboard](https://www.instagram.com/accounts/insights/?timeframe=30), account `@jacobychye`.

## Reporting period and definitions

- Dashboard filter: **Last 30 days**, inspected on 15 October 2026.
- Instagram's information panels say today is excluded and calculations use Pacific Standard Time. The corresponding activity period is **15 September–14 October 2026**.
- Followers are the total reported when checked on **15 October 2026**. Instagram warns that follower numbers may be delayed.
- Views count plays/displays, not unique people, and include crossposted Facebook views. Viewers are **estimated Meta accounts that viewed content at least once**. They are not labeled as Instagram-only reach.
- Content interactions and accounts engaged retain the dashboard's definitions, including boosted content/ads. The account dashboard supplies no platform split for these metrics. Do not characterize them as an independently summed Instagram + Facebook total or as entirely organic.
- No engagement rate is published: the former rate and reach/follower ratio were removed rather than mixing incompatible populations.
