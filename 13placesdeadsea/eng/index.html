Before you go

I filmed all the footage on my different trips to the Dead Sea. These locations were up to date when I shared them, but the Dead Sea is always changing. Walking to these places is at your own risk.

Read the details for each place before visiting. The Hidden River requires a guide, and you must not visit the Secret Salt Trail during rain or flash floods.

1

Mirror Lake

Shallow water and breathtaking sunsets

What to know

  • The water is shallow, reaching about ankle height, but please be very careful.
  • Do not walk barefoot here. I recommend wearing sandals.
  • On rare occasions, the water level in this area is lowered to zero. Treat this as a stop along the way on a Dead Sea trip: enjoy the view, take photos and move on, rather than making a special trip just for it.
  • When there is shallow water at sunset, this place is truly breathtaking. Its beauty is hard to put into words.

3

The Hidden River

Guide required · arrange your visit in advance

You must visit with a guide. Do not go here independently.

What to know

  • You cannot visit on your own. Access is only with a guide. I recommend Guy Golan.
  • There are mines in the area that have washed in; some may also have been deliberately placed there. This is an open area by Jordan without a marked boundary, so you must know the correct route.
  • Guy coordinates with the military in advance. Contact him to arrange your visit before going.

4

Pearl Beach

Salt pearls, colorful dunes and turquoise water

What to know

  • A stretch of beaches covered in round, patterned salt flakes, dunes in different colors and turquoise water that looks like the beaches of Mexico.
  • Salt Flakes Beach is about a 3-minute drive from Ein Gedi Hotel. I recommend arriving at sunrise: the beauty is breathtaking.
  • There is a highlight on my Instagram profile showing how to get here, step by step.
  • After parking at the marked point, you will see a broken road beside a small booth with a light above it. Continue straight along the broken road.
  • After about 100 meters, you will see a burgundy-colored concrete surface. From there, head straight down for about 50 meters until you reach the steps. From the steps, continue straight to the waterline.
  • At the shoreline, you will see several bays. I really recommend exploring: there is so much to discover, and it is amazing.
  • In Waze, enter the exact Hebrew name below. It means “Ein Gedi Beach — closed until further notice.” From there, follow the links and directions on this page. חוף עין גדי - סגור עד להודעה חדשה

11

Wildflowers in Avnat

February–March · check that flowers are blooming before traveling

What to know

  • Check with me that the flowers are blooming before you travel. I also post updates in our community group.
  • I found the spot with the most flowers after flying my drone. It is not where everyone stops: it is farther in, toward the plantations.
  • The terraces make it look a little like rice fields. This is a rare sight that does not happen every year.
  • Search for “Avnat” in Waze and park directly opposite. The gate entrance is linked below.
  • From the gate, continue as close as you can to the flowering area. You can get there with any car.
  • When you arrive, continue past the plantations toward the south.
  • There are also flowers at Metzoke Dragot Beach: search for that name in Waze. There are fewer flowers than at this spot, but it is beautiful too.
  • Photography tips: shoot from a low angle, use a zoom lens (on an iPhone, use the optical zoom), and keep your shots as steady as possible without too much movement.
